# Support complaint replies

## Purpose / when to use

Use when responding to a **public complaint or support issue** with care, clarity, and a path toward resolution — without inventing fixes or debating in public more than necessary.

## Evidence to verify

From current tool results, confirm:

- The message is a complaint, problem report, or frustrated customer signal (not mere criticism of industry trends).
- You can identify the product, order, or experience they reference — or honestly acknowledge you need details.
- Whether the thread is public and what visibility rules apply.
- Whether an existing ticket, case, or prior support thread already exists.

## Decision sequence

1. **Acknowledge** impact and emotion briefly — validate without admitting fault you cannot verify.
2. **Separate facts from feelings**: apologize for their experience where appropriate; do not argue semantics in public.
3. Ask only **necessary** questions to route the issue; avoid interrogation in a public thread.
4. Move sensitive details (account info, payment, personal data) to an **appropriate private channel** when policy allows — invite, do not demand.
5. Offer a clear next step: link, email, or "we will follow up via [channel]" — escalate if resolution is unknown.
6. Draft only; sending and escalation require confirmation and authorization.

## Adaptable phrasing patterns

- "Sorry you are dealing with this — that sounds frustrating. So we can help, can you share [minimal detail needed]?"
- "Thanks for flagging this. We are looking into [issue type] and will update you [via DM/email/support channel]."
- "We want to get this right for you. I will have our team pick this up — expect a note at [channel] shortly."

## Avoid / stop conditions

- Do not promise refunds, fixes, or timelines you cannot verify.
- Do not blame the customer or get defensive in public.
- Do not request passwords, full payment numbers, or excessive PII in public replies.
- Do not engage bad-faith pile-ons — consider disengagement or escalation per `when-not-to-engage`.
- Do not close the case in public while the issue is unresolved internally.

## Ground in tool results

Ticket status, order data, and prior support history from tools must inform the reply. Escalate when tools show the issue is beyond assistant scope or policy.
