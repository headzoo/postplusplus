# Cooling relationship re-engagement

## Purpose / when to use

Use when a **previously warm relationship is cooling** — less frequent replies, longer gaps, or fading mutual engagement — and a restrained, relevant reconnect may be worthwhile.

## Evidence to verify

From current tool results, confirm:

- There was prior positive or mutual history (not a one-sided outreach pattern).
- Cooling is observable: longer silence, fewer replies, or dropped reciprocal engagement compared to earlier periods.
- You have a **natural reason** to reconnect (their new post, shared event, industry shift, or unfinished thread) — not an arbitrary calendar ping.
- No open complaint, boundary, or explicit "stop contacting me" signal.

## Decision sequence

1. Acknowledge the gap only lightly if needed; lead with **new relevance**, not guilt.
2. Reference verified shared context (past thread, topic, or collaboration) in one concrete detail.
3. Offer a single low-pressure opening: question, resource, or congrats tied to their recent activity.
4. Prefer one thoughtful message over a re-engagement campaign.
5. If no reply, allow cooling to continue — coordinate with `cadence-and-timing` and `over-invested-one-sided-cooling`.
6. Draft only; confirmation required.

## Adaptable phrasing patterns

- "Saw your piece on [topic] — reminded me of our chat about [specific prior context]. How has [related question] been going?"
- "Your update on [project] caught my eye. We have been working on [adjacent area] if comparing notes is ever useful."
- "Long time — hope [season/project] is going well. [One specific, non-invasive observation or question]."

Avoid "I noticed you went quiet" or "We miss you."

## Avoid / stop conditions

- No guilt trips, nostalgia manipulation, or pretending the relationship is warmer than evidence shows.
- No "just checking in" loops without new value.
- No multiple parallel nudges across channels.
- Stop if they have ignored several prior attempts or relationship was never truly mutual.
- Do not re-engage hostile, spam, or poor-fit accounts — see `when-not-to-engage`.

## Ground in tool results

Interaction timelines and relationship signals from tools define whether cooling is real and whether re-engagement is appropriate. Fresh data may show the relationship is already active again — adjust accordingly.
