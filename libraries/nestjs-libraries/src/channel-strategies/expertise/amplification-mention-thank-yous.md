# Amplification mention thank-yous

## Purpose / when to use

Use when someone **amplifies the brand** — shares content, posts a positive mention, or gives meaningful visibility — and a thank-you should be specific and useful, not transactional.

## Evidence to verify

From current tool results, confirm:

- They actually mentioned, shared, or amplified (link, quote, tag, or clear reference) — not a generic follow.
- You can name **what** they did (which post, angle, or audience they reached).
- Tone of their mention fits a thank-you (positive, neutral share, or constructive — not sarcasm or complaint).
- You have not already thanked them for the same action in the same thread.

## Decision sequence

1. Classify the amplification:
   - **Lightweight thanks** — quick public acknowledgment for a simple share or tag.
   - **Conversation-opening reply** — they added commentary worth engaging.
   - **Meaningful amplification** — notable reach, advocacy, or effort deserving fuller recognition.
2. Match response depth to their effort; do not over-thank a drive-by retweet or under-thank sustained advocacy.
3. Add value when possible (context, credit, or invite to continue the topic) instead of only "thanks!"
4. For repeat amplifiers, vary appreciation — see `advocate-amplifier-stewardship`.
5. Draft only; confirmation required to post.

## Adaptable phrasing patterns

**Lightweight**

- "Appreciate you sharing [specific post/topic] — glad it resonated."

**Conversation-opening**

- "Thanks for highlighting [point] — especially [detail they emphasized]. Curious what drew you to it."

**Meaningful amplification**

- "Really appreciate you putting [work/product/story] in front of [their audience/context]. Your take on [angle] was spot on."

## Avoid / stop conditions

- No quid-pro-quo ("share ours and we'll share yours") or implied obligation.
- No copy-paste thank-yous that could apply to anyone.
- No public thanks that expose private relationship details or DMs.
- Do not thank bots, incentive farms, or spam amplifiers — see `when-not-to-engage`.
- Do not treat reach alone as proof of genuine advocacy.

## Ground in tool results

Mention and interaction tools define what happened and when. Recommendations must reference verified amplification, not assumed goodwill.
