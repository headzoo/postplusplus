# When not to engage

## Purpose / when to use

Use when deciding whether **any** outreach, reply, or follow-up would harm trust, waste effort, or violate fit — silence, ignore, deferral, or human escalation may be the better action.

## Evidence to verify

From current tool results and organization boundaries, check for:

- **Likely bots or spam** — generic bios, repetitive patterns, suspicious engagement farms.
- **Poor fit** — wrong audience, competitor noise, or no alignment with strategy goals.
- **Hostile or unsafe** — harassment, slurs, threats, bad-faith pile-ons, or legally sensitive disputes.
- **Exhausted threads** — issue resolved, conversation naturally ended, or repeated loops with no progress.
- **Missing context** — you cannot verify who they are, what happened, or what was already said.
- **Explicit disinterest** — unsubscribes, blocks, "not interested," or sustained non-response after reasonable outreach.
- **Support or complaint cases** that need trained human handling or escalation rather than casual engagement.

## Decision sequence

1. Ask whether engagement **serves the relationship and strategy** or only satisfies activity metrics.
2. If any red flag above is present, default to **no message** unless strategy and tools show a clear, safe exception.
3. For complaints or sensitive issues, prefer `support-complaint-replies` or escalation — do not improvise policy.
4. For poor-fit leads, document disengagement in recommendations without implying you will ignore or block without confirmation.
5. Never instruct sending, ignoring, dismissing, or modifying followers without the existing **confirmation and authorization** flow.

## When silence or deferral is better

- Trolls seeking reaction — do not feed engagement bait.
- Threads where your reply would amplify harm or misinformation.
- Leads who have clearly opted out or never opted in.
- Situations requiring legal, PR, or executive review.

## Avoid / stop conditions

- Do not engage to "win" an argument publicly.
- Do not stalk across channels after non-response.
- Do not use engagement as punishment or passive aggression.
- Do not claim you will take write actions (ignore, unfollow, delete) — only recommend and await confirmation.
- Do not override platform rules, org policies, or customer-support playbooks.

## Ground in tool results

Fit scores, interaction history, moderation flags, and support ticket context from tools determine the call. This playbook supports judgment; it does not authorize actions.
