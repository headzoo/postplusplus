# Advocate and amplifier stewardship

## Purpose / when to use

Use when nurturing **repeat advocates and amplifiers** — people who consistently share, defend, or promote the brand — so appreciation stays genuine and sustainable over time.

## Evidence to verify

From current tool results, confirm:

- A pattern of advocacy beyond a single mention (multiple shares, referrals, testimonials, or sustained positive engagement).
- What they value: topic expertise, community, early access, recognition — infer only from public signals and past interactions.
- You have not over-thanked the same action recently — coordinate with `amplification-mention-thank-yous`.
- Reach is not the only signal; quality and authenticity of support matter.

## Decision sequence

1. **Recognize consistently** but vary wording and format so thanks do not feel automated.
2. Occasionally deepen the relationship: ask their perspective, share relevant news first, or invite input — not only extract promotion.
3. Invite participation **without** demanding free labor (posts, reviews, events) as entitlement.
4. Credit them appropriately when resharing their content; do not use their name for marketing without alignment.
5. Steward long-term: advocates cool too — apply `cooling-relationship-reengagement` if pattern fades.
6. Draft only; confirmation required for any outreach.

## Adaptable phrasing patterns

- "Your ongoing support on [topic] means a lot — especially [specific recent example]."
- "Before we publish [thing], would your take on [angle] be useful? No pressure either way."
- "Wanted you to see [update] first given how much you have championed [area]."

Rotate between public shout-outs and private notes based on what fits their style and prior exchanges.

## Avoid / stop conditions

- No transactional rewards language ("post again and we'll…") unless org policy explicitly supports a formal program.
- No treating amplifiers as unpaid marketing staff by default.
- No public praise that reveals private relationship details or internal metrics.
- Do not rank advocates only by follower count — see genuine engagement quality in tool history.
- Do not authorize gifts, discounts, or contracts without proper approval and confirmation.

## Ground in tool results

Advocacy history, mention frequency, and relationship trajectory from tools guide stewardship intensity. Strategy may span retention and brand awareness — align tone with the active server-resolved strategy.
