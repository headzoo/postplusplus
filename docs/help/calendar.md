# Calendar

The Calendar is where you plan, review, and publish posts across your channels. Open it from the **Calendar** item in the main navigation.

This is different from the [Dashboard](/help/dashboard), which shows analytics—not your schedule.

## Using the Calendar

You’ll see a grid (day, week, or month) or a list of posts, plus search and optional channel group filters. Click a future empty slot or the header **Post** button to open the composer. Past slots are striped and can’t receive new posts or drops.

## Views: Day, Week, Month, and List

- Switch between calendar and list with the view icons.
- In calendar mode, choose **Day**, **Week**, or **Month**, jump with prev/next, or click **Today**.
- In list mode, filter with **All**, **Scheduled**, **Draft**, or **Published**, and move through **Page X of Y**.

Empty list messages include **No upcoming posts scheduled**, **No draft posts**, **No published posts**, and **No posts**.

## Scheduling

1. Click **Post** in the header, or click a future cell on the grid.
2. If you use Sets, you may see **Choose a set or continue without one**—pick a set or **Continue without set**.
3. Select the channel circles you want to publish to.
4. Write your content and attach media as needed.
5. Choose how to publish:
   - **Schedule manually** — pick a date and time, then **Add to calendar** or **Schedule**.
   - **Post Now** — publish immediately.
   - **Pipelines** — add to a Pipeline queue without picking a date (see [Pipelines](/help/pipelines#queue-content-without-picking-dates)).
6. Or **Save as Draft** / **Save** to keep work in progress.

Primary button labels change with the mode (**Check the circles above**, **Add to calendar**, **Schedule**, **Post Now**, **Add to Pipeline**, **Update**, and others).

You need at least one connected channel. Without channels, creating from a slot opens the add-channel flow instead.

## Edit, duplicate, preview, and delete

On a post card (hover for actions):

- Open the post to edit it in the composer.
- **Duplicate Post** creates a copy (often into a new slot).
- **Preview Post** opens a shareable preview; **Open Post** opens the live platform URL when available.
- **Post Statistics** shows engagement when the platform supports it.
- **Delete Post** asks for confirmation, then removes the post.

Published posts on some networks can’t be edited in Post++ after they go live.

## Move a post to a new time

Drag a post to another future cell. You can’t drop onto past hours.

If the post was already published or is past a queue state, you may see **What do you want to do?** with:

- **Just update the post details**
- **Reschedule the post** (may republish to the channel at the new time)

Pipeline items on the calendar can also be dragged; doing so may pin or schedule them outside the queue.

## Search and filter

- **Search by title or content**, then **Search**—results open in list view. Clearing search restores your previous view.
- If you have multiple channel groups, **Select Channel Group** limits which channels and posts you see (toast: **Channel group selected**).

While searching, list state chips (**All** / **Scheduled** / **Draft** / **Published**) are hidden.

## Channels, sets, and signatures

- Connect channels from the Channels sidebar (**Add Channel**) or related menus elsewhere in the app.
- **Sets** are reusable multi-channel templates you manage under [Settings](/help/settings#sets-and-signatures).
- A default signature may append automatically when you create a post without a set, if you’ve configured one in Settings.

Channel **Edit Time Slots** (from the channel menu) influences how “find next slot” suggestions work when creating posts.

## Pipelines on the calendar

Queued Pipeline items can appear on the calendar as projected posts. To enqueue from the composer, choose a Pipeline under **Pipelines** and click **Add to Pipeline**. Learn more in [Pipelines](/help/pipelines).

## Troubleshooting

- **Timezone** — times display in your local/browser timezone. A mismatch can make slots look “wrong.”
- **Past slots** — you can’t create or drop there; schedule for the future.
- Red **!** — the post hit a publish error; open it to inspect and fix.
- **This post was deleted on the platform** — the live post is gone; the calendar still shows history.
- **Connect Post** — appears when the live release link is missing; use it to reconnect the platform post.
- X statistics may be unavailable depending on your workspace configuration.
