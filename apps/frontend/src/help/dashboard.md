# Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen for channel performance. Open it by clicking the Post++ logo. It shows analytics for **one connected channel at a time**—not your post schedule. For planning and publishing, use the [Calendar](/help/calendar).

## What the Dashboard shows

After you pick a channel in the **Channels** sidebar, the main area shows stat cards for that channel: totals, trends, and charts over the selected time range. Metrics depend on what the platform provides, so the cards you see can differ by channel.

## Connect and pick a channel

1. In the **Channels** sidebar, click **Add Channel** if you have none yet.
2. Complete the connection flow for your provider.
3. Click a channel in the list to load its analytics.

If you see **No channels yet**, add a provider from the sidebar before charts can appear. Your last selected channel is remembered the next time you return.

From a channel’s menu you can also **Create a new post**, reconnect or update credentials, edit time slots, enable or disable the channel, and more. Disabling or deleting a channel may require cleaning up related posts first.

## Choose a time range

Use the **7 days**, **30 days**, or **90 days** toggle to change the window on every visible chart. The default is **7 days**.

## Customize your stats

- Remove a card with **Remove stat**.
- Bring hidden metrics back with **+** (**Add hidden stats**). If nothing is hidden, you’ll see **No hidden stats**.
- Drag cards to reorder them.

Your layout is saved **per channel**. If every card is hidden, you’ll see **All stats are hidden. Use the + button to add them back.**

## Collect analytics when data is missing

When history is incomplete, use **Collect analytics**. Collection can take a few minutes; the page polls until data arrives.

- You may see **Analytics collection started. This may take a few minutes.**
- For X (Twitter), charts may need **two** collections before they appear.
- If the channel needs a reconnect or refresh, collection stays blocked until you fix the connection.

## Open a specific day

Click a bar on a chart to open that metric for that date. You’ll leave the overview and land on a focused day view for the selected channel and metric.

## Channel problems

| Message                                                 | What it usually means                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **No analytics data for this period**                   | Nothing recorded for this range yet—try collecting or a wider range.                |
| **Analytics not supported**                             | This provider doesn’t expose analytics in Post++.                                   |
| **Reconnect or refresh this channel to view analytics** | OAuth or credentials need attention.                                                |
| **Channel disabled**                                    | The channel is turned off; enable it from the channel menu if you want stats again. |

## Related: create a post

Use the header **Post** button (when you have at least one channel) or a channel’s **Create a new post** menu item. Scheduling and drafts live on the [Calendar](/help/calendar#scheduling).
